# flatline play-test round, 2026-09-04: the record, the ending, the fighter who ends on the main line

Three scripted personas driven through the real dispatcher (harness.py, spine.py,
pa_record.py, pb_ending.py, pc_fighter.py in this directory; full transcripts in
logs/). Five campaigns, about 1,700 turns, no crashes. Every persona used its own
XDG_DATA_HOME under data/, so nothing here touched the real profile.

## In order of size

1. The main line's posting outlives its own ending (B1, B2, B3, and C).
   The offer stage takes any_of (dw_pattern, dw_carried), so the three facts
   alone bring the offer while "Four Hundred and Eight" is still on the board,
   held and non-expiring. After the ending it stays there (`*1 Four Hundred…
   held`), and if it was accepted it stays the current job: B3 refused the
   offer on shift 17 and `now` still said "On the job: Four Hundred and Eight,
   next jack in" on day 12. C reached the offer via the pattern, refused it,
   finished the posting afterwards, and got "You read it, or you do not" as a
   stray scene after the ending.
   Fix: when an ending flag lands (City._settle_deepwater or the offer's
   resolve), withdraw the deepwater.posting contract from the board, clear
   `accepted` if it is that one, and say so once on the wire. The carried and
   under stages are foreclosed on that path, which is coherent: the offer came,
   you answered, the record with your name goes away.

2. "Afterwards" opens in the same breath as the ending, not a few shifts later
   (all four endings). Story._old_enough for a thread's first stage only
   measures from a `met:` rule; the three openers have none, so `after=3` is a
   no-op and "On the inside of it" prints directly under "9,000c in."
   Fix: for a first stage with no met:, measure from `when['thread:<owner>']`
   of the thread that set the flag it requires (`_flag_owners()` already knows).

3. The name the city calls you is stuck on catalogue order (A, C).
   world/record.title_of returns the last earned title in ENTRIES order, not
   the last earned. A was "who asks" on `char` through six later names
   (a working runner, quiet, still here, in the middle of things, who stands
   about, who knows people); C stayed "on the wall" after "who went and looked".
   The announcement says one name and `char` says another.
   Fix: order titles by meta['recorded'] (the order they landed); section
   titles and COMPLETE still last.

4. The ambition "What Deepwater is" is done the moment you hear the name.
   content/ambitions.py: predicate is `'dw_heard' in flags`. With D143 the
   thing is finding out. Fix: `dw_pattern` (or any ending flag).

5. A fighter's experience is advised as `spend`, never `train violence` (C).
   44 shifts on the wall (rung 4, 13 wins) at Violence 1 with 22 xp in hand;
   every `now` said `spend` (which proposes Warfare and Forensics for an

## Observations, not bugs

- The posting is a posture-72 large Deepwater network at runs:5. Two of three
  who took it came out with nothing on the first try; the fighter finished it
  on the second. The pattern path gets the same ending, so nobody was stuck.
- Dissonance stayed at 7 across 33 shifts and 15 runs for a chrome-free
  runner. "Deepest drift 60" and "Runners who decided about you" were the two
  lines a 33-shift achiever could not move; everything else on the record moved
  or landed (10 of 25 by shift 33, 16 of 25 counters moved).
- A fighter build finished 5 of 5 runs at Intrusion 1 to 2. The pivot is viable.
- The record announced every line exactly once across all five campaigns, and
  a second character on the same terminal saw the record carried over.
- All four offer endings were reached by play (take, publish, refuse x2), each
  settled the city once, the Deepwater palette unlocked on the first shift
  after, and after_rest fired in all four.
- Shifts to an ending: 20, 20, 17 (net-first), 67 (42 as a fighter first).
